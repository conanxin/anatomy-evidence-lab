REFERENCE / 一手定义

NIST Dictionary of Algorithms and Data Structures: B-tree ↗。节点多路搜索、同深度叶节点、有限节点容量。这里可切换最小度数 t=2 或 t=3；各节点最多 2t−1 个键、内部节点最多 2t 个子节点。

NIST Dictionary of Algorithms and Data Structures: B-tree ↗ defines balanced multiway search trees. Our teaching convention compares minimum degree t=2 and t=3: up to 2t−1 keys and 2t children per internal node.
